# Third-party corpus notice

This app can fetch a Hebrew word corpus at runtime from:

`eyaler/hebrew_wordlists`, specifically `hspell_simple.txt` through GitHub raw content.

The repository states that Hspell and the generated dictionary word lists are licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License version 3 (AGPL-3.0), and that the lists come without warranty. The full corpus is not bundled here; the app fetches it only when the device has network access. A tiny local fallback list is included for offline play.

Because AGPL obligations can matter if you redistribute modified versions or bundle the corpus, treat this as a notice rather than legal advice.
